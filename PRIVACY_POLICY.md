# QuickNote Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [date]_

QuickNote stores everything on your device. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and doesn't ask for internet access.

**What is stored:** your notes (title, text, color, pinned and private flags, timestamps), your theme and default-color settings, and — if you use private notes — encrypted note data plus key material in Android's secure storage. Your PIN is never stored.

**What we collect:** nothing. We don't receive, see or share your notes or any personal data.

**Private notes:** encrypted on your device. If you forget your PIN they can't be recovered.

**Deleting your data:** delete notes in the app, or uninstall the app, which removes all stored data.

**Links:** the "Follow the developer" button opens Instagram in your browser or Instagram app; their privacy policy applies there.
